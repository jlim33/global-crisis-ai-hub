# 🌍 CrisisPulse (글로벌 크라이시스 & AI 펄스)
### Global Conflict, Climate Disasters & AI Planetary Solutions Hub

*Real-time intelligence platform monitoring AI military warfare, climate disasters, and worldwide humanitarian AI recovery efforts.*

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities

1. **🛰️ Planetary Situation Room Aesthetic**: Arctic command glass UI with crimson threat alerts & luminous cyan recovery indicators.
2. **📡 Live Global Threat Ticker**: Real-time ticker for autonomous drone warfare, cyber incidents, super typhoons, wildfires, and UN AI relief missions.
3. **📊 Threat Level & AI Role Diagnostic Engine**:
   - Threat Level: `Level 1: Critical Emergency 🚨` to `Level 4: Relief in Progress 🕊️`
   - AI Dual-Role Assessment: `Autonomous Weaponry ⚔️` vs `Humanitarian AI Rescue 🌐`
4. **🕊️ Peace & Relief vs 🚨 Emergency Alert Voting**: Community engagement sentiment gauge bar.
5. **🎙️ US Native Voice Situation Briefing**: Real-time audio playback in natural American broadcast voice.
6. **🌐 Dual Language Architecture**:
   - 🇰🇷 Korean Edition: `/` (글로벌 크라이시스 & AI 펄스)
   - 🇺🇸 English Global Edition: `/en` (CrisisPulse)
7. **🔔 Planetary Situation Report**: Daily briefing dispatcher via Formspree.
